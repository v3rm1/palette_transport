These re-render without re-solving. Smoothing runs a guided filter on the displacement field T(x)−x, guided by source lightness, so flat regions like sky stop banding while edges keep their colors.

Convergence

Gaussian OT applies the closed-form map between Gaussian fits first: T(x) = μ̄ + A(x − μₓ), onto the Gaussian W₂ barycenter of the references, or onto the exact mean and covariance of the mixture. The sliced iterations then only correct the non-Gaussian residual. The dashed line is the previous run, for comparison.
